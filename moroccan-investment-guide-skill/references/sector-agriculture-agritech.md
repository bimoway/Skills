# Agriculture, Agri-food & Water-Tech — Génération Green 2020-2030

> Framework: *Stratégie Génération Green 2020-2030 (Ministère de l'Agriculture); Loi n° 63-18 (projet, foncier agricole — se référer au texte promulgué au Bulletin Officiel); Dahir du 25 juillet 1969 + Code des Investissements Agricoles; Loi 36-15 sur l'Eau; Loi 82-21 sur l'autoproduction électrique / PPP dessalement. Opérateurs: SOGETA, SODEA, ORMVA, ADA, ONSSA.*

## 1. Génération Green 2020-2030 Framework
Successor to Plan Maroc Vert. Two pillars + transversal digital:

1. **Priorité à l'élément humain** — 350k+ new jobs target, young agri-entrepreneurs (*entrepreneuriat agricole des jeunes*), cooperatives, social protection for farmers, *classes moyennes rurales*.
2. **Pérennité du développement agricole** — 1M ha in direct seeding, efficient irrigation (goutte-à-goutte), certification, e-commerce souks, doubling agri-exports.

Investor hooks: aggregation projects (*agrégation*), cold chain, packing houses (*stations de conditionnement*), seed/seedling labs, smart irrigation, soil/crop data, traceability (GlobalG.A.P., SMETA), carbon/water credits.

Aid channels: **Fonds de Développement Agricole (FDA)** — subsidies via ADA/ORMVA dossiers (e.g., 30-70% for drip irrigation, wells with solar, cold storage, planting — rates by arrêté, verify). Charter Prime sectorielle agroalimentaire adds on top for processing (cumul rules).

## 2. Agricultural Land Rules for Foreign Entities — Foncier Agricole
**Rule: foreigners cannot acquire full ownership (*pleine propriété*) of agricultural land outside urban perimeters.** Acquisition by foreign natural/legal persons of *terres agricoles* is restricted (Dahir 1969 / Code rural + jurisprudence + circulars). Workarounds — all via State domains:

### SOGETA / SODEA Long-Term Leases
- **SOGETA** (*Société de Gestion des Terres Agricoles*) and **SODEA** (*Société de Développement Agricole*) manage State private domain lands.
- Instrument: **bail emphytéotique** (long-term emphyteutic lease) typically **17 to 40 years** (up to 99 years in historic texts — current practice 17-40 ans, renewable), registered at Conservation Foncière, mortgageable (*hypothéquable*) and transferable with State approval.
- Tender (*appel à projets / appel d'offres*): business plan, capex/ha, jobs, water plan, export/processing commitment. Rent (*redevance annuelle*) per ha by zone/irrigation; revision clauses.
- Conditions: personal exploitation obligation, no subdivision without approval, forfeiture (*déchéance*) if unexploited 2+ years, pre-emption State on transfer.

### Other routes
- Lease from private Moroccan owner: **bail à ferme** (rural lease, flexible 6-20+ years) — allowed for foreigners as tenants, not owners. Notarize + register.
- Urban/peri-urban industrial land for processing plant: full ownership via ZAI/industrial lots allowed (see industry file) — separate from farmland rule.
- Collectives lands (*terres collectives / soulaliyates* — Loi 62-17, 63-17, 64-17): melkisation/lease via tutelle Interior — long, avoid unless partnered with local counsel.

> Due diligence: *Titre Foncier*, vocation agricole certificate, ORMVA water rights, drilling authorization (Agence de Bassin Hydraulique), ONSSA phytosanitary constraints.

## 3. Agri-food Processing Incentives
- FDA + Charter Prime sectorielle: packing, freezing (*surgélation*), oil/olive mills, dairy, canning, juice, couscous/pasta.
- Export support: Maroc Export/AMDIE fairs, EU Association + UK + AfCFTA preferences if rules of origin met; ONSSA + EU SPS approvals needed (HACCP, IFS/BRC).
- Cold-chain corridors: Agadir–Casablanca–Tanger Med reefer logistics; Nador West Med future outlet.

## 4. Desalination, Water-Tech & Green Hydrogen Priorities
Water stress + 6-year drought sequence makes water the binding constraint.

- **Dessalement:** Casablanca (300 Mm³/an), Agadir (irrigation + drinking), Dakhla, Nador mega-plants — PPP/ONEE/OCE models. Irrigation reuse (*réutilisation eaux usées traitées*) prioritized by Loi 36-15.
- Investor plays: drip tech, solar pumping with smart metering, desal O&M, membranes, wastewater treatment (STEP), water auditing, drought-resistant seeds.
- Tariffs: subsidized agricultural water vs cost-reflective desal water — model blended price with ORMVA contracts.
- **Hydrogène vert / Green Hydrogen:** *Offre Maroc* (1M ha reserved, Guelmim-Oued Noun first tranche): electrolyzers, ammonia/methanol for fertilizers (OCP ecosystem), e-fuels. Land via Circulaire Chef du Gouvernement + MASEN/ONEE PPAs. Requires EIE + grid/dessal water coupling.
