# Industrial Ecosystem: Automotive, Aerospace, Textiles — Zones d'Accélération Industrielle (ZAI)

> Framework: *Loi n° 19-94 relative aux Zones Franches d'Exportation (Dahir n° 1-95-1), refondue vers le régime des Zones d'Accélération Industrielle (ZAI); Loi-cadre 03-22 (Prime sectorielle/territoriale); Code du Travail; normes environnementales. Opérateurs: Tanger Med Zones, MEDZ, CRI. À vérifier au Bulletin Officiel.*

## 1. Industrial Acceleration Zones (ZAI) — Offer
Former *Zones Franches d'Exportation* rebranded as **Zone d'Accélération Industrielle (ZAI)**. Key parks:

| Park | Region | Positioning |
|---|---|---|
| **Tanger Med Zones** (Tanger Free Zone, Tanger Automotive City TAC, Tétouan Park) | Tanger-Tétouan-Al Hoceima | Automotive, aeronautics wiring, textiles; adossé au Port Tanger Med (1er Afrique) |
| **Tanger Tech / Cité Mohammed VI Tanger Tech** | Tanger | Ville industrielle sino-marocaine, EV/batteries, électroménager |
| **Midparc + Aéropole Nouaceur** | Casablanca-Settat (Nouaceur) | Aerospace — Boeing/Safran ecosystem |
| **Casablanca ZAI** (Ain Sebaâ, Bouskoura, Ouled Salah) + **Atlantic Free Zone Kénitra** | Casablanca / Rabat-Kénitra | Stellantis Kénitra + suppliers, electronics |
| **Agadir ZAI + Fès/Meknès parks** | Souss / Fès-Meknès | Agro-industrie, cuir/textile, câblage |

**ZAI advantages:** customs suspension (*admission en franchise* for inputs re-exported), simplified customs on-site (*dédouanement à domicile*), one-stop administration, built-ready lots/halls (*bâtiments prêts à l'emploi*), IS/tax incentives via Charter + Loi de Finances (verify year — historically 5-year IS exemption then reduced rate for exporters), training pools OFPPT-IFA.

Access: lease/purchase via aménageur (MEDZ/TMSA) + CRI/CRUI authorization. Export obligation historically ≥70-85% turnover for free-zone status — verify current ZAI cahier des charges (increasing domestic-market allowance).

## 2. Automotive — Renault / Stellantis Hubs
Morocco: 1M+ vehicles/year capacity, top African producer/exporter to EU.

- **Renault Tanger Melloussa** (~340k cap.): Dacia Sandero/Jogger, Logan; local sourcing ~60%+.
- **Stellantis Kénitra** (~200k→400k extension): Peugeot 208, Citroën Ami, Fiat Topolino; Atlantic Free Zone suppliers (Forvia, Yazaki, Sumitomo, Lear).
- **Ecosystems:** wiring (*câblage*), stamping, plastics, seats, batteries/EV (Gotion High-Tech Kénitra gigafactory project, BTR cathodes).
- Investor entry: Tier 2/3 (plastic injection, metal stamping, molds, PCBs), EV components, aftermarket. Requires IATF 16949, capacity audits by OEMs.

**Local integration rules (*taux d'intégration locale*):** OEM price agreements reward rising local content (target 65-80%). Charter Prime + Fonds de Soutien Automobile bonuses tied to integration and jobs. Non-tariff: EU-Morocco Association Agreement — duty-free access if EU rules of origin met.

## 3. Aerospace — Midparc Nouaceur Model
- Anchor: Boeing, Safran (nacelles, wiring), Spirit AeroSystems, Sabca/GKN, Hexcel composites.
- Activities: aerostructures, engine components, MRO, engineering (mathematics/CATIA talent via ENSAM/EMI + IMA Casablanca).
- Certifications mandatory: EN 9100 / AS9100, NADCAP for special processes, EASA Part 21/145 for MRO.
- Midparc offer: tax/customs ZAI + bonded logistics + on-site aerospace training institute (IMA). Land: long-term lease with extension options.

## 4. Textiles & Leather — Repositioning
- Zones: Tanger-Tétouan (fast-fashion nearshoring Zara/Inditex), Casablanca-Settat denim, Fès leather.
- Shift from CMT to co-contracting/full-package + technical textiles (automotive airbags, medical). Compliance: EU due-diligence (CSRD/CSDDD pass-through), ZDHC wastewater.
- Incentives: Prime sectorielle textile-habillement + training aid (OFPPT textile centers).

## 5. Green Energy & Decarbonization Requirements
EU CBAM (*Mécanisme d'Ajustement Carbone aux Frontières*) hits Moroccan steel, aluminium, cement, fertilizers, electricity exports from 2026. OEMs (Renault/Stellantis/Airbus) impose Scope 3 CO₂ targets.

Implications:
- New factories must plan: rooftop PV + PPA renouvelable (ONEE/Masdar frameworks), energy audit, ISO 50001/14001, bilan carbone.
- Industrial wastewater + waste traceability; EIE (*Étude d'Impact Environnemental*) mandatory for classified establishments (*établissements classés*) via CRUI.
- Green hydrogen pipeline (see agriculture file) opens decarbonized steel/ammonia plus EV-battery green premium.

## 6. Practical Setup Steps in ZAI
1. RFI to aménageur (MEDZ/Tanger Med Zones) + CRI: lot size, power (MW), water, effluents, ceiling height.
2. CRUI file: EIE, fire (*protection civile*), urban permit.
3. Customs registration as *opérateur en ZAI* + bonded stock IT (BADR system).
4. Charter grant application in parallel (Prime territoriale + sectorielle).
5. OFPPT staffing convention + ANAPEC recruitment wave.
