# Foreign Investor FAQs & Incorporation — Propriété, Change, Comptes, Fiscalité, Emploi

> Framework: *Code de Commerce 15-95; Lois 5-96 (SARL) / 17-95 (SA); Loi 89-17 (succursales — vérification BO); Instruction Générale des Opérations de Change (IGOC) de l'Office des Changes; Code Général des Impôts (CGI); Code du Travail + arrêtés ANAPEC; circulaires Bank Al-Maghrib sur les comptes convertibles. Guichets: CRI, OMPIC, DGI, Office des Changes, ANAPEC. À vérifier au Bulletin Officiel.*

## FAQ 1 — Can a foreigner own 100% of a company in Morocco? (Propriété étrangère à 100%)
**Yes — in almost all sectors.** (*Oui, propriété à 100% possible.*)

- No general foreign-ownership cap. SARL, SA, SAS-equivalent (SA simplifiée in practice), branch (*succursale*), liaison office (*bureau de liaison* — non-commercial) all open to 100% foreign capital.
- Exceptions / prior approval (*agrément préalable*): banking/insurance (BAM/ACAPS license), telecom spectrum (ANRT), gaming, arms/explosives, maritime/air transport cabotage, media quotas, farmland ownership (see agriculture file — lease only), and regulated professions (avocat, notaire, architecte — nationality/bar requirements).
- Incorporation path (via CRI/OMPIC):
  1. **Certificat négatif OMPIC** (name reservation).
  2. **Statuts** (notarized for SA/real-estate contributions; sous-seing privé possible for SARL cash) + **blocage du capital** attestation (bank).
  3. **ICE** (*Identifiant Commun de l'Entreprise* — 15 digits), **IF** (*Identifiant Fiscal*), **TP** (*Taxe Professionnelle / patente*), **CNSS** affiliation.
  4. **Immatriculation au Registre du Commerce** + **annonces légales (JAL) + Bulletin Officiel**.
- Minimum capital: SARL 10,000 DH (liberation flexible); SA 300,000 DH (3 MDH if APE/listed). Timeline 1-3 weeks if file clean.

## FAQ 2 — How does FX convertibility work? (Régime de convertibilité + rapatriement garanti à 100%)
Morocco has controlled convertibility, but **foreign investments funded in foreign currency enjoy 100% guaranteed repatriation** (*garantie de transfert*).

- Legal base: **IGOC — Instruction Générale des Opérations de Change** (Office des Changes). Operations via **intermédiaire agréé** (licensed bank).
- Covered (*investissements étrangers au Maroc*): capital contributions, capital increases, shareholder loans in FX (*comptes courants d'associés en devises* if declared), dividends (*dividendes*), sale proceeds / capital gains (*produits de cession / plus-values*), liquidation boni.
- Procedure:
  1. Inward transfer with SWIFT motif investissement + **Fiche de déclaration d'investissement** via bank to Office des Changes (within deadlines — typically 30 days / online reporting).
  2. Keep OMPIC/RC + bank credit advices (*avis de crédit*) proving FX origin.
  3. Annual audit + dividend distribution (AGO minutes) → bank executes transfer without prior Office approval if file clean (ex post control).
- Portfolio (CSE shares) same logic if funded via convertible account.
- Moroccan-dirham-funded (non-convertible) reinvested earnings: repatriation capped/scheduled — structure initial funding in FX to preserve full convertibility.
- Current account operations (imports, services, expat salaries) liberalized via dotations (commerce extérieur, voyages, e-commerce caps) — via bank on documentary proof.

## FAQ 3 — How to open a Convertible Dirham Account? (Compte en Dirhams Convertibles)
- **Compte en Dirhams Convertibles** (and **Compte en Devises** for FX retention) opened with any intermédiaire agréé upon: passport, proof of address, RC/ICE for companies or incorporation pack, Office des Changes investment declaration, KYC/AML (source of funds).
- Functioning: credited in FX converted to DH (or held in FX); debited freely outward (dividends, imports, salary transfers) and locally. No prior Office authorization for assured-transfer operations.
- Variants: personal convertible account for expatriate staff (*transfert épargne sur salaire* — typically up to % of net salary), professional account for entity, *compte-titres* cash pocket linked to brokerage.
- Practical tip: open pre-incorporation blocked-capital account (*compte de blocage*) then convert to operating convertible account post-RC.

## FAQ 4 — Corporate Tax (IS), VAT on Setup, and Incentives
- **Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS)** — standard rate historically 35% top slice / 31-35% for financial/large, 20% intermediate, with reduced/export/CFC/ZAI tracks. **Finance Laws 2023-2026 unify progressively toward 20% base + 35% for high-profit/regulated** — MUST verify Loi de Finances of the year at DGI/Bulletin Officiel. Minimum contribution (*cotisation minimale* 0.25-6% by margin) applies if loss-making (verify current rate/exemptions for new companies — typically 3-year exemption for newcos).
- **VAT (TVA)** — 20% standard; reduced 7/10/14% by product. **Exonération/suspension TVA sur biens d'équipement** during incorporation/investment program (24-36 months, renewable once) for eligible investment accounts — file *demande d'exonération* with DGI + Douane before import/acquisition; equipment must be booked as immobilisations + non-transferable N years.
- Other: **Taxe Professionnelle** (exemption 5 years for newcos), **Taxe de Services Communaux**, registration duties (*droits d'enregistrement* 1-6% on asset deals), social charges CNSS (~21.09% employer) + AMO.
- Withholding on dividends (see CSE file): 15% standard, treaty relief.

## FAQ 5 — Hiring Foreign Staff (ANAPEC / Autorisation + Ta3teed-type contracts)
- Any foreign employee needs: **contrat de travail étranger visé** + **autorisation ANAPEC** (*attestation ANAPEC* proving no qualified local profile for the post after job posting) → then **permis de travail** (Ministère de l'Emploi) + **titre de séjour** (DGSN) for residence.
- CFC entities and Charter strategic projects: fast-track / reduced labor-market test (see services file).
- Contract types: CDI (standard), CDD, **contrats ANAPEC Tahfiz/Idmaj** (wage/training subsidies for young graduates — employer CNSS/IR breaks for 24 months), *contrat intérim* via agencies.
- Employer duties: CNSS declaration, AMO, IR withholding (*retenue à la source sur salaires*), work regulations (*règlement intérieur*) if ≥10 staff, elected delegates (*délégués du personnel*), annual medical.
- Termination: strict cause + conciliation/inspection du travail; severance scales with seniority; expatriate repatriation ticket + end-of-assignment transfer of savings via convertible account.

## One-Page Incorporation Checklist
- [ ] Certificat négatif → Statuts → Blocage capital → ICE/IF/TP/CNSS → RC + JAL/BO
- [ ] FX: inward SWIFT + investment declaration via intermédiaire agréé (Day 1)
- [ ] DGI: IS/TP/TVA elections + equipment VAT-exemption request before import
- [ ] ANAPEC: post jobs → foreign-staff attestations → work permits + séjour
- [ ] Post-closing: CRI follow-up for Charter Prime tranches; custodian/broker for CSE leg

*Information générale, non constitutive d'une consultation juridique/fiscale. Vérifier CGI, IGOC et Code du Travail consolidés au Bulletin Officiel et consulter CRI + intermédiaire agréé + avocat/expert-comptable.*
