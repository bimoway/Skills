# New Investment Charter Incentives — Charte de l'Investissement (Loi-cadre n° 03-22)

> Applicable law header: *Loi-cadre n° 03-22 formant Charte de l'Investissement, promulguée par Dahir n° 1-22-76 du 14 joumada I 1444 (8 décembre 2022), publiée au Bulletin Officiel. Textes d'application: Décret n° 2-23-1 et arrêtés conjoints (primes, seuils, secteurs). À vérifier au Bulletin Officiel — montants et listes de provinces actualisés par voie réglementaire.*

## 1. Architecture of the Charter
The Charter replaces the 1995 Charter (Loi-cadre 18-95). Three pillars:
1. **Prime à l'investissement** — direct State grants (subventions) paid after verification, not upfront tax holidays alone.
2. **Appui territorial via CRI/CRUI** — single-window approval and land facilitation.
3. **Conventions d'investissement** — contractual State commitment for large/strategic projects + improved guarantees (transfert, protection, règlement des différends).

Eligible: Moroccan and foreign natural/legal persons, MRE, new projects AND extension projects meeting thresholds. Excluded de facto: pure commercial trading without transformation/value-add, and sectors on negative lists per decree.

## 2. Primary Direct Investment Grants — up to 30%
**Rule:** Total State grant capped at **30% of eligible investment amount** (*montant d'investissement éligible*). Paid in tranches against milestones (réalisation vérifiée).

### 2.1 Prime commune (common grant) — 5 building blocks
Cumulative within the 30% cap:

| Component (*Prime*) | Criteria | Indicative rate logic (verify decree) |
|---|---|---|
| **Prime territoriale** (*territorial grant*) | Location in Category A (most disadvantaged provinces/prefectures, e.g., interior, mountain, Saharan provinces) vs Category B (intermediate) vs other zones | Up to ~15% (Cat. A) / ~10% (Cat. B) of eligible capex |
| **Prime sectorielle** (*sector grant*) | Priority sectors: industry, tourism, digital/offshoring, logistics, renewable energy, R&D, culture/creative, health/education, agri-food processing | Up to ~7-10% depending on sector list |
| **Prime emploi** (*employment grant*) | Number of stable jobs (*emplois stables* — CDI, CNSS-registered) created; bonus for women/youth share | Scales with jobs (e.g., >150 jobs = higher tier); ~5% range |
| **Prime genre / formation** | Share of women employees, vocational training (*formation professionnelle*) spend | Complementary points within cap |
| **Prime développement durable / intégration** | Local integration (*taux d'intégration locale*), decarbonization, energy efficiency, export share | Bonus points |

> Practical example: 100 MDH factory in Category A (15%) + priority industry (7%) + 200 stable jobs (5%) + gender/sustainable bonus (3%) = 30% → 30 MDH grant, capped at 30%.

### 2.2 Prime additionnelle territoriale
Extra grant for projects in least-developed provinces (list by decree) — on top of Prime commune but still within overall 30% ceiling unless classified strategic.

### 2.3 Projets à caractère stratégique
Strategic projects (defense, pharma/vaccines, semiconductors, green hydrogen/ammonia, EVs/batteries, water/desalination mega-projects): negotiated package via **Commission Nationale des Investissements** chaired by Head of Government — can combine land at preferential terms, infrastructure co-funding, training support, plus Prime.

### 2.4 Thresholds (verify current decree)
- General regime: typically ≥ 50 MDH investment AND ≥ 150 stable jobs (lowered for SMEs in some territories — e.g., ≥ 10-15 MDH in Category A).
- SME track: specific support for TPME with reduced thresholds via CRI.
- Convention d'investissement mandatory above threshold; below threshold, simplified CRI decision.

Eligible costs (*assiette éligible*): land (capped), construction, new equipment (hors taxes), R&D capex, training capex. Excluded: working capital (*besoin en fonds de roulement*), used equipment, pure real estate speculation.

## 3. Approval Institutions: CRI & CRUI

### Centre Régional d'Investissement (CRI) — Regional Investment Center
- 12 CRIs (one per region). Guichet unique: company creation support, land identification, file assembly, post-approval follow-up.
- Receives grant application (*demande de Prime à l'investissement*), checks completeness within days, transmits to CRUI.

### Commission Unifiée Régionale d'Investissement (CRUI) — Unified Regional Investment Commission
- Single decision body chaired by Wali. Merges former commissions (investments, land, urban planning derogations).
- Decisions: urban/land authorizations + eligibility opinion within statutory deadlines (target 30 days). Silence rules and digital tracking via platform CRI-Invest.
- Above national threshold → forwards with opinion to Commission Nationale des Investissements for Convention signature with the State.

### Workflow for investor
1. Pre-filing at CRI: business plan, capex breakdown, jobs plan, site shortlist, environmental note.
2. File Convention/grant request → CRUI instruction → decision + draft Convention.
3. Sign Convention d'investissement with State (Ministère délégué chargé de l'Investissement).
4. Realize → independent verification → disbursement in tranches (typically 2-4 versements) to company bank account.
5. Clawback (*reversement*) if jobs/capex commitments not met within monitoring period (usually 5 years).

## 4. State Subventions & Complementary Aids
- Customs duty exemption on imported capital goods (*exonération droits d'importation biens d'équipement*) during setup (verify Code des Douanes + Loi de Finances year).
- VAT suspension/exemption on capital goods imports for eligible investment accounts.
- Vocational training support via OFPPT/ANAPEC contracts.
- Land incentives: ZAI lots at incentive prices, long leases, PPP for industrial parks.
- Regional Council co-funding possible in addition to State Prime (cumul rules apply).

## 5. Guarantees for Foreign Investors
- Freedom of establishment (except regulated sectors), 100% ownership allowed (see investor-faqs).
- Free transfer of capital, profits, dividends if investment routed via foreign currency + declared to Office des Changes (IGOC).
- Protection against expropriation except public utility with fair compensation; Morocco BITs + FTAs (EU, US, AfCFTA) + CIRDI/ICSID membership for dispute settlement.

## 6. Checklist & Pitfalls
- [ ] Confirm province Category (A/B) in latest joint arrêté — determines 10% vs 15% territorial leg.
- [ ] Structure capex to maximize assiette éligible (new equipment, not BFR).
- [ ] Lock jobs definition: CDI + CNSS, maintained N years.
- [ ] Never start construction before filing — anteriority principle (expenses before receivability may be excluded).
- [ ] Budget 3-6 months CRI→Convention→visa; grant paid ex post, so secure bridge financing.
