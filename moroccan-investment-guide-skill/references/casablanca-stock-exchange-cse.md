# Casablanca Stock Exchange (CSE) — Bourse de Casablanca: Market Structure, MASI, Equities & FX

> Framework: *Loi n° 44-12 relative à l'Appel Public à l'Épargne (APE); Loi n° 43-12 relative à l'AMMC; Règlement Général de la Bourse des Valeurs (RGBV) approuvé par le Ministre des Finances; circulaires AMMC; IGOC Office des Changes; Code Général des Impôts (dividendes); Loi 19-14 relative à la Bourse (gestionnaire). Regulator: Autorité Marocaine du Marché des Capitaux (AMMC). Operator: Bourse de Casablanca. Custodian: Maroclear. À vérifier au Bulletin Officiel.*

## 1. Capital Market Structure Regulated by AMMC
- **AMMC** (*Autorité Marocaine du Marché des Capitaux*): grants **visa de la note d'information** (prospectus visa) for any APE/listing, supervises disclosure (*information financière*), insider/market abuse (*délit d'initié / manipulation de cours*), licenses **sociétés de bourse** (brokers) and **sociétés de gestion** (asset managers).
- **Bourse de Casablanca** (listed itself since 2016 — ticker BVC): electronic NSC V900 trading, 9:30-15:30 Casablanca time, pre-open/pre-close auctions.
- **Maroclear**: central depository (*dépositaire central*), delivery-versus-payment, T+2.
- Indices publisher: CSE + FTSE Russell partnership for tradable families.

Retail access only via licensed **société de bourse** or bank-intermediated order; foreign remote membership possible via local custodian (*conservateur*) + intermédiaire agréé for FX.

## 2. Benchmark Indices: MASI & MASI20
- **MASI** (*Moroccan All Shares Index* — *MASI flottant*): capitalization-weighted free-float index of all listed shares. Base 1000 (31/12/1991). Review semi-annual.
- **MASI20**: top-20 most liquid blue chips (liquidity + float screen). Used for index funds/derivatives reference.
- Others: MASI ESG, sector indices (Banques, Télécoms, BTP, Mines), MSI20, Casablanca ESG 10.
- Performance anchor: banks (~30-40% of cap) + telecoms + materials drive MASI; dividend yield historically 3-4%+.

Process for non-resident:
1. Open **compte-titres** with société de bourse + cash account linked to **Compte en Dirhams Convertibles** (see investor-faqs).
2. FX funding via intermédiaire agréé with Office des Changes declaration (investment in foreign currency = repatriation-guaranteed).
3. Place order (limit/market); broker routes to CSE; execution → confirmation (*avis d'opéré*).
4. **Règlement-livraison T+2** (*settlement T+2*): Maroclear DVP; custody in investor name.
5. Ongoing: dividends credited net of withholding; annual tax certificate (*attestation*) for home-country treaty claim.

Portfolio convertibility: capital + gains + dividends 100% repatriable if originally funded in foreign currency and declared — Instruction Générale des Opérations de Change, rubrique Investissements étrangers au Maroc (actions cotées).

## 4. Listing Requirements — Main vs Alternative (SME) Market
Any listing requires AMMC visa + CSE admission.

|  | **Marché Principal** | **Marché Alternatif (PME)** |
|---|---|---|
| Target | Large caps | SMEs/ETI growth |
| Min. free float / public spread | Higher (e.g., ≥ 20% or ≥ 75 MDH market float — verify RGBV) | Lower (e.g., ≥ 10% or smaller ticket — verify) |
| Track record | 3 years certified accounts, profits history | 2 years, lighter profit condition + business plan |
| Sponsor | Listing sponsor (société de bourse) + commissaire aux comptes | Mandatory **conseiller en cotation / listing sponsor** for 3+ years post-listing |
| Disclosure | Full IFRS prospectus + quarterly | Simplified note d'information + ongoing sponsor certification |
| Free float liquidity | Market maker recommended | Liquidity contract (*contrat de liquidité*) often required |

Steps: board/AG approval → appoint société de bourse + legal/auditors → draft note d'information → AMMC visa → CSE admission committee → IPO (*OPV — Offre Publique de Vente*) or direct listing → first quotation (*première cotation*) → ongoing disclosure (faits importants, semestriels/annuels).

Costs: AMMC/CSE/Maroclear fees + sponsor + legal/audit/communication (~3-6% for SMEs).

## 5. Dividend Withholding Tax & Capital Gains
- **Retenue à la source sur dividendes** (*dividend withholding*): standard 15% for residents/non-residents (verify Loi de Finances — progressive alignment 2023-2026 discussions; treaties e.g., France 15%, Spain/UK/Gulf vary 5-15%). Local custodian withholds; treaty relief via *attestation de résidence fiscale*.
- Capital gains on listed shares: regime depends on holding/residence — Moroccan individuals historically with exemptions/deferrals for listed shares; foreign portfolio investors: check treaty + IGOC repatriation net of tax. Always confirm current CGI article with DGI circular.
- No stamp duty on secondary trades for investor (broker commission ~0.1-0.6% + CSE/Maroclear micro-levies + 10% VAT on commissions).

## 6. T+2 Settlement, Custody & Risks
- T+2 DVP via Maroclear; fails trigger buy-in procedures per RGBV.
- Risks: liquidity concentration (few names = 60%+ volume), FX (dirham band ±2.5% vs EUR/USD basket), withholding drag, corporate governance (controlling families).
- Data: cours at casablanca-bourse.com; filings at ammc.ma.
