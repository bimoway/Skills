# Services: Offshoring, IT, Tourism & Casablanca Finance City (CFC)

> Framework: *Loi-cadre 03-22 (Prime sectorielle digital/tourisme/finance); Loi n° 44-10 relative au statut CFC (modifiée par Lois de Finances); Code du Tourisme (Loi 80-14); Code du Travail; IGOC Office des Changes; circulaires DGI. Opérateurs: CFC Authority, AMDIE, CRI, ANAPEC. À vérifier au Bulletin Officiel.*

## 1. Offshoring Hubs — Nearshore Proposition
Morocco = leading francophone + emerging anglophone nearshore (time zone GMT+1, 1-2h from EU).

### Sites
- **Casablanca Nearshore / Shore Park (Sidi Maârouf), Technopolis Rabat/Salé, Fès Shore, Tétouan Shore, Agadir Shore** — dedicated office parks with telecom redundancy, 24/7 access, training centers.
- Talent: ~15k+ engineers/year, French/Arabic + growing English/Spanish; attrition lower than Eastern Europe.

### Segments
- ITO (dev, cloud, cybersecurity, data/AI), BPO (customer service, back-office finance), KPO (accounting, actuarial), ESO (engineering/automotive embedded).
- Anchor clients: Capgemini, Atos, Accenture, Webhelp/Concentrix, Dell, Orange Cyber.

### Incentives (verify Loi de Finances in force)
- Charter Prime sectorielle digital/offshoring + Prime emploi (large CDI volumes score high).
- Historically: IS advantages for export services + training grants via ANAPEC/OFPPT conventions (*contrats de formation-insertion*).
- Telecom/data: ANRT licenses, data protection Law 09-08 (CNDP) — EU adequacy effort; SCCs for EU transfers.

Setup: SARL/SA + CRI file + ANAPEC mass-recruitment wave + lease in shore park (plug-and-play floors). Export turnover in foreign currency via intermédiaire agréé → convertibility benefits.

## 2. Tourism Incentives — Cap 2026 / Vision 2030 (Mondial 2030)
Strategy Cap 2026 → Vision 2030 (17.5M tourists target, 2030 FIFA World Cup co-host).

- Priority: resorts (Taghazout, Saïdia, Dakhla lagoon kitesurf), medina riads, eco-lodges Atlas, MICE Casablanca/Marrakech, health/spa.
- Vehicle: **Société de Développement Touristique** + SMIT (Marocaine d'Ingénierie Touristique) land bank + PPP.

## 3. Casablanca Finance City (CFC) — Statut CFC
Financial hub in Anfa (Casablanca) with continental Africa mandate. Status granted by **CFC Authority** commission after eligibility review.

### Who qualifies
- Financial institutions (banks, insurers, asset managers, PE/VC), regional HQs (*sièges régionaux*), holdings, professional services (law, audit, consulting) serving Africa, fintechs (with BAM/AMMC license path), shared-service centers of multinationals.

### Tax status benefits (verify current Loi de Finances — historically, summarizing)
- **Corporate Tax (IS):** historically 5-year full exemption (*exonération quinquennale*) from first profitable year or grant date, then reduced rate (e.g., 15% capped) for ongoing CFC activities — MUST verify current rate as Finance Laws 2018-2024 narrowed then stabilized CFC regime for OECD/EU compliance (substance requirements).
- Personal income for expatriate staff historically with advantages + social security totalization options.
- Customs facilitation for setup equipment.

> Critical: post-BEPS substance test — real office, local hires, board meetings in Casablanca, audited Africa-sourced revenues. CFC Authority annual compliance review; loss of statut CFC if substance fails.

### Streamlined Work Permits & FX
- Facilitated **permis de travail / titres de séjour** for foreign executives and families via CFC one-stop + Ministère de l'Emploi/ANAPEC (reduced TA3TEED-style labor-market test for CFC entities).
- FX: CFC entities benefit from IGOC flexibilities — foreign-currency accounts, free cross-border intra-group invoicing via intermédiaire agréé, easier expatriate salary transfer (*transfert des salaires*).

### Application steps
1. Eligibility memo to CFC Authority (activity, Africa scope, jobs, substance).
2. Incorporation (SA/SARL/SAS-equivalent) + lease in CFC zone.
3. CFC commission decision → *Attestation statut CFC* → file with DGI for tax election + Office des Changes for FX elections.
4. Annual reporting to CFC Authority + statutory audit.
